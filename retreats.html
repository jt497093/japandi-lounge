What to expect

Space to properly recover

Japandi Lounge is a restored Brabant farmhouse surrounded by peaceful countryside near ’s-Hertogenbosch.

The house combines Japanese minimalism with Scandinavian warmth, creating a calm environment for rest, reflection and meaningful time together.

The aim is simple: create enough distance from daily routines to slow down, recover and return with more energy and clarity.

The programme

Experiences built around recovery

Retreat programmes can combine guided sessions with quiet time, shared meals and use of the house and wellness facilities.

01

Breathwork

Focused breathing sessions designed to create space, settle the mind and reconnect with the body.

02

Ice-bath sessions

Guided cold exposure in the peaceful outdoor setting of Japandi Lounge.

03

Sound healing

Slow down and settle into a quieter state with sound-based relaxation sessions.

The stay

Retreat in comfort

The retreat experience continues between sessions. Japandi Lounge offers indoor and outdoor spaces designed for slowing down, recovery and connection.

  • Sauna
  • Wood-fired hot tub / jacuzzi
  • Outdoor fire pit
  • Large communal kitchen
  • Long dining table for shared meals
  • Indoor and outdoor lounge areas
  • Peaceful countryside near Den Bosch
